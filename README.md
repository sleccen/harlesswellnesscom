# Harless Wellness Hub

make a modern, professional chiropractic landing page website for this chiropractor using the given information where needed:

Jack M. Harless BS, DC

Treating Physician

Dr. Jack M. Harless is a 1993 graduate of Centerville High School & currently resides there with his wife, Julie, & four children; Olivia, Anna, Elias & Emmry. He is a third generation chiropractor who has grown up with vitalistic & wellness concepts & is excited to help others live & implement the wellness lifestyle.

Dr. Harless graduated from Logan Univeristy with a BS in Life Sciences and a Doctorate of Chiropractic degree from Logan College of Chiropractic in St. Louis MO. Dr. Harless also holds a license to practice physical therapy by the State Board of Ohio. Dr. Harless hold certifications in Level 1 Rehabilitative Qigong & Tai Chi & Rehabilitative Qigong for Pulmonary Health from the Institute for Rehabilitative Qigong & Tai Chi (IRQTC), as well as is a certified Healer Within Medical Qigong practice leader from the Institute of Integral Qigong & Tai Chi (IIQTC).

Dr. Harless has given 300 + hours of wellness education lectures in his 23 years of experience in the health and wellness field and has studied and implemented the Epstein model of health & wellness with an emphasis on Reorganizational Healing & quality of life social science indexes, which have allowed his patients to achieve new and exciting levels of health and well-being. He is currently serving as treating physician at Holten Wellness Center & is excited to bring a dynamic adjusting processes that facilitates greater levels of function and health & healing to our wellness community at Holten Wellness Center.

In his free time, you can find Dr. Harless coaching for Centerville High School Varsity Lacrosse.
